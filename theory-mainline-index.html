4. Shared-interest hooks

Questions only, no manifesto. In conversation, pick 2–3.

  1. Records before observers? If records/correlations make outcomes definite, what counts as a “record” before any observer? (→ 2.2)
  2. Does information change the reachable future set, or only the description? Are there distinguishable consequences? (→ 2.3)
  3. Is the classical world an error-correcting code? Coarse-graining drops information “lawfully”; redundant records remain — where does this break first? (→ T3 / 2.6)
  4. Are relations more basic than objects? Which concrete physics phenomenon gets clearer under morphisms and composition? (→ 2.5 / T4)
  5. Is it-from-bit ontology or bookkeeping? Wheeler’s participatory universe: ontology or epistemic convenience? What would a discriminating experiment look like? (→ 2.2)
  6. Encoding ≠ ontology: what does a simulation drop? Homomorphic but non-isomorphic simulation — what is lost, and does it matter? (→ §3)
  7. Copy ≠ continuity: after a perfect copy, what carries “you” without soul-talk, using only information and correlation? (→ §3)
  8. What would falsify an information-ontology claim? Willing to require explanation gain beyond a naïve frame? (→ §3)
  9. Self-correction under measurement: maintaining structure while being measured — more physics or more cognition? (→ 2.7 adjacent)
  10. Which non-mainstream entries brought you here? Entry videos/podcasts as triggers OK, as arguments not — which of yours survives that demotion? (→ 2.7)
