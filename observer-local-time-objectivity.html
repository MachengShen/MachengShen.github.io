No shared clock ≠ no shared world

One line. Relativity removes a global simultaneity clock, not a shared world. Objectivity is better read as cross-observer invariants plus emergent consensus from redundant records. Consciousness is not promoted to ontology.
Provenance and honesty. This page absorbs a 2026-09-24 hard-theory review (Claude Fable 5.1, Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Opus 4.6 on one claim pack). The owner delegated two definitional gates to the fleet; the fleet ruled by shared logic and wrote the result into the unified index. This is not a final physics theorem and not an unbounded endorsement of the spoken claim. Private chat dumps, internal drop ids, hosts, and keys do not appear here.

1. Original conjecture (before narrowing)

  1. Time may be constructed by an observer inside a local coordinate system, not a shared global axis.
  2. If everything is structure and information, “the objective world” may be a mistaken approximation.
  3. Even granting locality, how large is a local patch? If every part influences every other, there may be no observer-independent objective world—only one unified system.
  4. Relativity’s non-shared time axis then looks natural: the physical world ≈ roughly compatible world-representations inferred by different conscious systems.

2. Steelman chain (what survives)

standing A · No global simultaneity clock

Special/general relativity abolish absolute simultaneity. This is textbook physics, not a consciousness thesis.

standing B · Cross-observer invariants remain

Frames still agree exactly on interval, causal order, proper time along a given worldline, and reunion outcomes. Disagreements follow exact transformation laws.

working C · Objectivity as emergent redundancy

Reread “objective” as structure that is invariant across recording systems and redundantly imprinted, not as a God-table of absolute simultaneous facts. Quantum Darwinism, relational stable facts, and contextuality (no global non-contextual value table) support degree-valued objectivity as an output.

working D · Observer = record/model system

Nail “observer” to a physical recording chain, not first-person experience—compatible with index 2.2.

speculative E · Time-from-correlation bridges

Page–Wootters-style constructions may illuminate local clocks; they do not by themselves erase a shared world.

speculative F · Holism metaphor only

“Parts and whole mutually specify” may be kept as relational metaphor; “part contains whole” collides with monogamy and no-cloning.

3. Stress tests (where the strong reading fails)

  1. Relativity is the wrong witness. “No shared time coordinate ⇒ no shared world” runs backwards.
  2. “Science’s basic assumption is wrong” is a strawman. Better: some approximations were mistaken for the fundamental level.
  3. Consciousness-as-constructor does not earn its keep against physical recording systems; it trips index guards.
  4. “Every part influences every other” confuses correlation with influence; light cones / no-signaling still bind.
  5. “Roughly compatible representations” need a mechanism; explaining agreement usually reimports shared structure.
  6. Collective subconscious “rendering” is a separate social-construction claim, not absorbed here.
