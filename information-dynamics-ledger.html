Information Dynamics: a future-reachability ledger

What this is

This is not a paper. It is a living index. It keeps what is standing, working, speculative, or already failed in the Information Dynamics / future-reachability line on one public page. Friends can enter here, check sources, and see which claims we no longer make.

Status legend

standing a fixed signpost of the current frame; working worth developing but still awaiting formal or empirical tests; speculative an exploratory bridge that evidence may demote; negative falsified or actively withdrawn. These labels describe our knowledge state, not nature's final verdict.

Public mainline claims

standing Information as an ontological entry point

We keep asking whether physical law can begin from distinctions, relations, records, and allowed transformations. This is a research frame, not the conclusion that “the universe is a computer”; it must earn its keep through explanatory gain and counterexamples.

working Records / correlations make outcomes definite

Stable traces can spread through an environment without waiting for a conscious observer. Wheeler's it-from-bit supplies the question; decoherence, decoherent histories, and quantum Darwinism supply more specific physical language. Together they do not prove one information ontology or automatically settle every measurement problem.

working Lawful update + change in the reachability set

A reachability set is the set of future states a system can actually still reach from its present state under given physical laws, resources, and constraints. The working hypothesis is that information, if more than description, changes this set through lawful, embodied, costly updates. Renaming the same possibilities does not count.

standing Encoding ≠ ontology; copy ≠ continuity

A model may faithfully encode some structure without being the same kind of thing as its target. Copying the same information does not by itself establish first-person or physical continuity. These are disciplines for discussion, not additional laws of the universe.

Speculative / known gaps

  • Constructor-theory bridge: Deutsch and Marletto formulate laws through possible and impossible transformations, which sits near reachability language. This site has not yet built the formal bridge. No affiliation, collaboration, or equivalence is claimed.
  • The Reachable World, fourteen pieces: the public arc is “what a state is → which paths constraints close → which futures remain open.” It is a continuous popular explainer; chapters are not yet individually online, and the definition of reachability is the one on this page.
