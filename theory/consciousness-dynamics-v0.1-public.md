# Consciousness Dynamics v0.1 — public digest

*Insight lineage: collaborator 球长一草; written up with Macheng Shen. Public digest 2026-09-18.*

## Status

- **Six realms as attractor modes:** `metaphor` (useful framing, not ontology of places after death).
- **Equations / RealmGate / W:** `design_hypothesis` — generative, untested as mechanism.
- **Not** clinical psychology. **Never** use realm labels to diagnose humans.

## Minimal picture

1. A mind-like system has modes that behave like attractors (threat, deficit-ingest, autopilot, competitive, glow-preserve, learnable “human” mode, etc.).
2. **W** = coarse awareness of “which mode-gate is firing.”
3. Low W → deepest available autopilot wins; practice ≈ see the gate before the old map runs.
4. This digest does **not** promote equations to convergent science. A poetic essay about planetary mind is **not** evidence for promotion.

## Coupling warning

Wanting both “no center” and “a planetary mind that can see its own gate” is a conjunction under tension: a mind that sees its gate **is** a center in this vocabulary; a hub with no W is a blackboard.
